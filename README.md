### ⚡ About Me

I am a 3rd-year undergraduate student at **GLA University** (CPI 8.7) and spent a semester as a visiting student at **IIT Gandhinagar**. I love tinkering with physical hardware: writing bare-metal C firmware, designing multi-layer PCBs in KiCad, and building robotic manipulators.

- 🔭 **Current Work**: Lead Hardware Engineer at **Dhritam** (point-of-care medical monitoring systems).
- 🤖 **Research Aspirations**: Preparing for **MS by Research in Robotics at IIIT Hyderabad**, followed by a Ph.D. in robotics.
- 🧩 **Problem Solving**: Solved **350+ problems on LeetCode** (algorithms, graph theory, dynamic programming).
- 🏆 **Hackathons**: National Finalist at **JPMorganChase Code For Good Hackathon 2026**.
- 🌐 **Portfolio**: [Explore my personal portfolio website](https://shubhamjain17r.github.io/Portfolio/)
